## Step 1: Identify the Big Problem
### Main Problem
The school canteen gets crowded during meal or break times, lines pile up because of many students have the same lunch times. This causes students to skip meals because of long waiting times and confusion.
---
## Step 2: Identify the Sub-Problems
1. Canteen gets crowded during break or meal times.
2. Staff gets overwhelmed and can't keep up with the orders.
3. Lines pile up because of many students with overlapping schedules.
4. Some students have to skip meals because they have to return for their next class.
---
## Step 3: Apply Computational Thinking Skills
| Sub-Problem | CT Skill | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Canteen gets crowded during break or meal times | Decomposition | Make multiple lines or queue lines to make the students organized |
| Staff gets overwhelmed and can't keep up with the orders | Pattern recognition | Prepare commonly ordered foods in advance |
| Lines pile up because of many students with overlapping schedules | Abstraction | Change schedules |
| Some students have to skip meals because they have to return for their next class | Algorithm design | Prioritize Students with less lunch time or break time |
---
## Step 4: Algorithmic Solution
### Some students have to skip meals because they have to return for their next class.
### Pseudocode
START
- Student enters the canteen
- Staff asks the students how long their lunch time or break time is
- Assign the student a queue number based on how long their lunch time or break time is
- Staff prioritizes students with less lunch time or break time
- Staff prepares student's food
- Staff notifies student when food is ready
- Remove the student on queue when food is ready

END
